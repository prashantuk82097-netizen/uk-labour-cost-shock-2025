# Results interpretation

## Primary result

The primary monthly fixed-effects coefficient is approximately **-0.00257 log points per +1 standard deviation of LCEI**. Under sector-clustered inference the estimate is not statistically distinguishable from zero.

The correct interpretation is:

> The sector-month analysis does not identify a statistically detectable post-April-2025 PAYE employment divergence that is proportional to the pre-policy LCEI.

It is **not** correct to say that the policy had no employment effect. The design does not isolate the separate causal effects of the NLW and employer NIC changes, the confidence interval contains modest negative and positive relative effects, and the joint pre-policy diagnostics limit causal interpretation.

## Why a null primary result is still informative

A weak aggregate headcount gradient is compatible with firms adjusting through other margins, including profit margins, prices, hours, recruitment, productivity, wage compression, workforce composition or investment. DMP and BICS evidence is used as secondary triangulation, not merged into the PAYE regression panel.

## Inference sensitivity

Most alternative specifications and covariance approaches do not reject the null. A Driscoll-Kraay-style covariance sensitivity is more negative/significant. Because statistical significance changes with the covariance estimator and pre-policy differences remain, the evidence is not robust enough to support a strong adverse causal-employment claim.
