# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-10-21

- Rebuilt the primary outcome panel at **monthly** frequency using every PAYE RTI month from January 2023 to July 2026.
- Retained the 2024 pre-policy LCEI construction unchanged.
- Established the 16-sector business-facing baseline (688 sector-month observations) and 19-sector robustness sample (817 observations).
- Re-estimated the two-way fixed-effects model, monthly event study, pre-trend tests, diagnostics and robustness suite.
- Added reproducibility tests, source register, data dictionary, model limitations and CI configuration.
- Added a quarterly sampling sensitivity derived from the same monthly panel to assess frequency dependence.
